The problem

Hardware supply data is everywhere. Reliability isn’t.

The parts you depend on are spread across manufacturers, regional distributors, specialist stores, and constantly changing product pages.

Different formats. Different currencies. Different regions. Different websites.

CANARY turns that fragmented web into one dependable source of hardware supply data.

Platform

From scattered supplier pages to one live supply layer.

01

Global coverage

CANARY collects hardware data across suppliers and regions instead of depending on a single marketplace or API.

02

One consistent format

Stock, price, availability, supplier, region, and provenance are normalized into a stable data contract that applications can depend on.

03

Built to survive website changes

When a supplier changes its page structure, CANARY detects broken extraction, repairs the collector, verifies the output, and continues using the same downstream contract.
